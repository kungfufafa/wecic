## Design Context

### Users
Primary users are CIC students using myCIC as a modern wrapper over the legacy campus portal. They typically access the product on phones and laptops between classes, while checking schedules, coursework, exams, profile data, attendance, and upload flows under time pressure. The core job is to complete routine academic tasks quickly and reliably without fighting the old portal's fragile UX or repeated login friction.

### Brand Personality
The product should feel academic, modern, and dependable. Its tone is precise, restrained, and typographically confident, with an institutional character that stays clear of bureaucratic heaviness. The emotional goal is to replace uncertainty from the legacy system with calm, trust, clarity, and a sense of operational control.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a light-mode-first system inspired by Linear's precision, but translated into CIC's academic identity. The interface should sit on a cool mineral canvas with crisp white panels, soft tinted surfaces, cool-gray borders, and measured elevation. Royal blue is the primary chromatic anchor for CTAs and active states, while gold is a controlled accent for moments of emphasis. Typography is centered on Inter Variable with `cv01` and `ss03`, using weight `510` as the signature emphasis weight and Berkeley Mono only for technical or code-adjacent content. Avoid dark-first treatments, muddy gray dividers, heavy shadows, decorative gradients, noisy card stacks, and overuse of blue or gold.

### Design Principles
1. Neutral surfaces should carry most of the interface, with brand color reserved for intent, hierarchy, and action.
2. The experience should feel institutionally credible without becoming stale, bureaucratic, or visually cold.
3. Dense, engineered typography should be balanced by generous light-space layouts and clear reading rhythm.
4. Borders define structure first; shadows only support separation when an element truly needs elevation.
5. High-frequency student tasks must feel immediate, legible, and reliable on both mobile and desktop.
